Talk to a counsellor
Are these Dolphin’s own videos?

The YouTube videos are: they come from Dolphin Education New Baneshwor’s own channel. The silent city clips are not; they are short stock clips by independent videographers, used under the free Pexels License, and each one names its creator.

Why are the clips silent?

They are short views of each city, about six seconds long, made to loop quietly. They have no sound track, so nothing plays aloud.

Why do the videos open on YouTube?

So this page stays light and loads nothing from YouTube until you choose a video. Each one opens on YouTube in a new tab.

Where can I read more about a destination?

Each destination page covers universities, costs, intakes and visas, and a free counselling session goes through your own options.
