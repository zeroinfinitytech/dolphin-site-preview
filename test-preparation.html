Three tests, one plan.

Universities and visa offices ask for different tests depending on the country and the course. Here is how the three compare, so your counsellor can help you choose before you start preparing.

British Council, IDP and Cambridge

IELTS Academic

The four-skill test most universities know best, with a speaking interview in person.

Suitsstudents who would rather talk to an examiner than to a microphone.

Format
On paper or on computer at a test centre. Speaking is a face-to-face interview with a certified examiner.
Sections
Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking, in about 2 hours 45 minutes.
Scoring
A band from 0 to 9 for each skill, and an overall band in half-band steps.
Results
Usually within a few days on computer, and in about two weeks on paper.
Accepted for
Study in the UK, Australia, New Zealand and Canada, and by many universities in the United States.

By Pearson

PTE Academic

A fully computer-based test, scored automatically, known for its quick results.

Suitsstudents who are comfortable on a keyboard and like a predictable, timed format.

Format
Entirely on computer at a test centre, including speaking into a headset. Responses are scored by AI.
Sections
Speaking and Writing, Reading, and Listening, in about two hours.
Scoring
An overall score from 10 to 90, with a score for each skill.
Results
Typically within a few days of the test.
Accepted for
Study in Canada, Australia, New Zealand and the UK, and by many universities worldwide. Always check each institution's requirement.

By ETS

TOEFL iBT

The long-standing choice for universities in the United States, taken on computer.

Suitsstudents applying mainly to American universities.

Format
On computer, at a test centre or at home with the Home Edition.
Sections
Reading, Listening, Speaking and Writing, in under two hours.
Scoring
The familiar total runs from 0 to 120. ETS has introduced a 1 to 6 band scale, so check which your university quotes.
Results
Online, usually within about a week.
Accepted for
Most common for universities in the United States, and accepted by many institutions in Canada, the UK, Australia and beyond.

Which test should I take?

It depends on the country, the university and sometimes the visa. Most universities accept more than one test, but requirements and minimum scores differ, so your counsellor checks each institution on your shortlist before you start preparing.

Is PTE easier than IELTS?

Neither is easier; they are different. PTE is entirely on computer and scored automatically, while IELTS has a face-to-face speaking interview with an examiner. Some students do better with one format than the other, and a free mock test is a good way to find out which suits you.

How long does preparation take?

It depends on your current level, your target score and how much time you can give each week. The diagnostic test at the start gives you a realistic estimate, and your mock test results show whether you are on track.

Can you book the official test for me?

We help you choose a suitable date and test centre and guide you through the booking. Test fees are set by the test provider, not by Dolphin.

How long is a score valid?

Most universities and visa offices accept IELTS, PTE and TOEFL scores for two years from the test date. Plan your test so the score is still valid when you apply and when you lodge your visa.
