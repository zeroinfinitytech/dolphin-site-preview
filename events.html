What happens at a session.

No sales pitch and no pressure: time with people who do this every day, and room for your own questions.

  1. Arrive

    Meet a counsellor

    A Dolphin counsellor walks you through the destination, the intakes and how applications actually work, step by step.

  2. Ask

    Costs and visas, plainly

    Tuition, living costs, the funds you must show and the visa process, with time to ask about your own situation.

  3. Hear

    From the university, where present

    At university visits, a representative explains courses, entry requirements and pathway options first hand.

  4. Leave

    With a plan

    You go home with clear next steps, and you can book a free one-to-one counselling session if you want to go further.

Before you come along.

Anything else? A counsellor can answer it in person in New Baneshwor or over the phone.

Talk to a counsellor
Are sessions free?

Information sessions are free to attend. If a session ever carries a charge, the listing will say so clearly before you register.

Do I need to register?

We recommend it. Registering lets us keep a seat for you and send you the details, and it matters most for university visits, where time with a representative is limited. Walk-ins are welcome when there is room.

Can parents come?

Yes. Parents and guardians are welcome at every in-person session. Questions about costs and funds are often easier to settle with the family in the room.

Are there online sessions?

Some do, such as the online IELTS session in May 2026; the joining link comes with your registration. If you cannot travel to an in-person session, a counsellor can also talk with you by phone.

What should I bring?

Nothing is required. For advice about your own plans, bring your latest academic transcripts, any English test results and a short list of questions.

Can’t make a date?

Register your interest and we will tell you about the next session that suits you, or book a free one-to-one counselling session instead.
